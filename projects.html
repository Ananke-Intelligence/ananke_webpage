Strategic Alignment with Artificial Mathematical Intelligence

Ananke Intelligence operates in close conceptual and research alignment with the global network of Artificial Mathematical Intelligence — an international initiative dedicated to advancing the foundations of cognitively grounded artificial intelligence.

While AMI focuses on the theoretical and formal development of cognitive–computational frameworks — particularly in the domain of mathematical reasoning and concept formation — Ananke serves as a translational and engineering counterpart, bringing these principles into real-world AI systems and industrial applications.

This connection enables Ananke to leverage a uniquely rigorous foundation: a structured understanding of how abstract concepts are generated, combined, and refined within scientific cognition. As a result, our data generation and model training methodologies are not only empirically effective, but also grounded in a deeper theory of intelligence itself.

Through this synergy, Ananke bridges frontier research and deployment, transforming advanced cognitive frameworks into scalable tools for next-generation AI systems.

PROJECT STRATA

The Mathematical Data Commons

Across the global research community, a critical bottleneck persists: brilliant minds with novel neural architectures and AI frameworks lack access to the structured, high-quality mathematical training data needed to test and validate their ideas. Only a handful of well-funded labs can run these experiments — leaving an enormous amount of intellectual potential untapped.

Project Strata is Ananke's answer to this gap. We are building a comprehensive mathematical dataset — organized not just by topic, but by levels of abstraction. This is not scraped math. This is math as intelligence infrastructure.

Researchers, institutions, and independent scientists will be able to take this structured data, configure it for their specific architectural experiments, and run the work that matters — without needing the resources of a frontier lab.

Our goal is to democratize access to rigorous mathematical AI training data, lowering the barrier to entry for the next generation of AI research.
